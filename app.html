Questions

Which NEC edition does VoltReady use?

References follow the 2023 NEC. Check which edition and exam rules your state uses.

Do I need my own code book?

Yes. VoltReady doesn’t include NEC text. The lookup trainer is built for practicing with your own book, just like an open-book exam.

Journeyman or master?

Both. Switch to Master mode for service, feeder, transformer and multifamily load calculations.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress stays on your phone. Pro purchases are restored through your Google Play account or Apple ID.
