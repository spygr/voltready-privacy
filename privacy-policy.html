VoltReady — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the VoltReady mobile app (“the App”) collects and how it is used.

In short
  • No account is required.
  • Your practice history stays on your device.
  • No ads, no analytics, and we don’t sell or share your data.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your study data in its local storage on your device: answers and results, timing, XP and streaks, exam history, flashcard progress, bookmarks, your exam date, daily goal and settings. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time from Profile → “Reset all progress”, by clearing the App’s data, or by uninstalling the App.

Purchases

If you buy VoltReady Pro, payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We don’t receive or store your payment card details. To confirm and restore purchases, the App uses a purchase-management service, RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, the products you bought and the store’s transaction receipts. It does not receive your name or email address from the App. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Analytics and advertising

The App does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Children

The App is a study tool for adults preparing for professional licensing exams. It is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Data deletion

Study data is deleted when you reset progress, clear the App’s data or uninstall it. To ask about purchase records, contact us at the address below.
