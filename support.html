VoltReady — Support

Electrician exam prep: NEC lookups, calculations, flashcards and timed exams.

Contact us

Email avdelasofia@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Please include your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Restore a purchase

Open the App, tap Go Pro (or Profile → Plans & restore purchases) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription (Yearly or Monthly)

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → VoltReady → Cancel Subscription.
Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → VoltReady → Cancel.

Reset progress

Profile → Reset all progress. This deletes your answers, XP, streak and exam history on this device. Purchases are not affected.

Which code edition does VoltReady use?

References follow the 2023 NEC. Always check which edition and exam rules your state uses.
